## Design Context

### Users

Farever players who need to understand short Portuguese chat messages while
remaining focused on active gameplay. The primary job is to scan recent chat,
translate only selected lines, and dismiss irrelevant messages quickly.

### Brand Personality

Compact, unobtrusive, and dependable. The translator should feel like a native
utility layered beside Farever rather than a separate desktop application.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark, low-contrast surfaces that remain readable over the game. Dense message
rows, small secondary controls, and restrained color distinguish sender,
original text, translation, and errors without competing with gameplay.

### Design Principles

- Preserve the game as the visual priority.
- Spend vertical space only on chat content and translation results.
- Keep the original and translation together in one quickly scannable unit.
- Make translation and dismissal available without large labeled buttons.
- Never steal focus, expose the cursor unexpectedly, or obstruct game input.
